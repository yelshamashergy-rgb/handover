Privacy Policy

Handover — Off-Plan Tracker · Last updated 8 June 2026

The short version

Handover does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no trackers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we store, and where

All information you add — properties, payment schedules, documents, notes, and settings — is stored locally on your device (in the app's own on-device storage). It never leaves your device except through actions you explicitly take, such as exporting a backup file that you choose to save or share, or — on iOS — being included in your own Apple iCloud device backup. We have no access to any of it.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or any personal information. We do not use third-party tracking.

Document attachments

Files you attach (e.g. a contract or certificate) are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

If you buy the optional Pro upgrade, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will reflect it.
